Why use the Business Model Canvas?
Describe a business model on one page Nine blocks cover who you serve, what you offer, how you deliver it, what it costs, and what it earns. Fill it once for one business model, not for the whole company.
See how the pieces depend on each other The right side is the market. The left side is what it takes to serve that market, with value propositions sitting between them. Move a customer segment and the channels, activities, and costs all have to move with it.
Treat it as a claim, not a plan Cost structure and revenue streams only hold if the seven blocks above them are right. Date and version the canvas, then redraw it as evidence comes in.
