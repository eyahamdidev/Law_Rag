Startup Tunisia’s programs
Startup Act
The Startup Act is a legal framework consisting of the three main texts. It integrates 20 measures structured around a Label of Merit and benefits to entrepreneurs, investors and Startups.
AIR
AIR is an instrument to support the development of Proof-Of-Concept, or first version of the innovative solution with high growth potential, for a Startup in very early stage.
AIR2
AIR² is a support for Startups that have raised SEED funds and are working towards a Scale up and Series A funding.
DEAL
DEAL is a grant for the launch of new programs to support startups, up to 200 000 TND.
ANAVA Fund of Funds
The Fund of Funds has a target size of €200M and aims to invest in more than 13+ investment funds dedicated to startups at each stage of development (Seed Stage, Early Stage and Late Stage).
SAIL
SAIL is a grant ensuring the continuity of certified SSOs based on the performance and impact achieved over the year.
The GP Incubator
A high-performance incubator to propel tomorrow's successful VCs
The Guarantee Program
Aligning the interests of entrepreneur & investors to allow good failure schemes
SAVE
A repayable advance to help startups impacted by the COVID19 pandemic to turn around their situation and save threatened jobs.
Ins & Outs of Ecosystem Building #02
Connection program between international experts and Tunisian SSOs.
Ins & Outs of Ecosystem Building #01
Connection program between international experts and Tunisian SSOs.
